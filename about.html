About

Delivery scope includes metafield and metaobject modelling, checkout functions, custom app workflow integration, and API-backed Shopify app integrations, alongside performance audits using Lighthouse and Core Web Vitals.

Currently operating within a Shopify Platinum Partner environment, focusing on high-impact e-commerce projects where architectural decisions support long-term business growth. Valuing clear ownership, thoughtful engineering, and systems designed to evolve beyond initial launch.

Particularly effective in environments where technical decisions have long-term commercial impact. Deeply committed to maintainability, clear ownership, and systems that withstand growth pressure. Not interested in short-term hacks that trade velocity for future rewrites.

Building scalable architecture and performance-led implementations for commerce platforms, specialising in Shopify web development with integrations via REST APIs, GraphQL, and third-party apps.

Working within mature delivery processes, contributing to architectural decisions, and maintaining high standards across code quality, performance, and maintainability using Git, Shopify CLI, and agile methodologies.
