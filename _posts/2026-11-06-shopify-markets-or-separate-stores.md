## The choice

When you start selling in more than one country, you can either run everything from one store, or open a separate store for each region. The decision affects how much work your team does every week, and it is expensive to undo, so it is worth thinking about early.

## One store

Choose this if your products are mostly the same everywhere, one team looks after everything, and you want the same look across every country.

- Products are updated once.
- Changes are made once.
- Customers and orders are in one place.

## Separate stores

Choose this if each region has a very different range, its own team, or its own company and finance set-up.

- Each region can work independently.
- Each store can be set up for its own market.

The cost is that more things are done more than once, and someone has to keep the stores consistent.

## Questions to ask yourself

- Is our range mostly the same in every country?
- Does one team manage all of it?
- Do we run everything through one company and one finance set-up?
- Do we want every country to look the same?

If most answers are yes, one store is usually simpler. If most are no, separate stores are worth a closer look.

## Plan for it early

Whichever you choose, the store should be built with the differences between countries in mind from the start. Adding them later costs more.
