This page is for store owners and ecommerce teams planning a move to Shopify or Shopify Plus. I work with you directly, from planning the move through to launch and the weeks after, so your products, customers and search rankings come across safely.

What a Migration Covers

  • Planning which pages, products and content move across
  • Setting up the new store and theme to match your requirements
  • Redirecting old addresses to the right new pages
  • Carrying over page titles and descriptions
  • Launch checks and monitoring in the weeks after
