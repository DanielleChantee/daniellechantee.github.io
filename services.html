Core Engagements

Shopify Plus Build and Architecture

Best for: New builds, major redesigns, and complex storefront re-platforming.

Typical scope: Theme architecture, reusable section/template systems, metafield/metaobject modelling, and integration-ready implementation.

Delivery model: Defined milestone delivery with clear technical ownership from scoping through production readiness.

Conversion and Reliability Stabilisation

Best for: Stores with friction in PLP/PDP/cart flows, inconsistent variant behaviour, or release-related regressions.

Typical scope: Conversion-path hardening, performance-aware frontend optimisation, integration conflict resolution, and checkout-adjacent implementation fixes.

Delivery model: Focused sprint engagements to stabilise revenue-critical journeys before major growth cycles.

Ongoing Implementation Partner

Best for: Product teams and agencies needing senior Shopify engineering support embedded into ongoing roadmap delivery.

Typical scope: Feature implementation, app/CMS integration work, checkout function updates, and release-safe technical iteration across live storefront systems.

Delivery model: Retained or sprint-based support with consistent handover quality and clear communication.
