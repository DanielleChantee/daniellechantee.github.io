Services

Shopify engineering for complex ecommerce teams

Shopify Plus engineering for ecommerce teams operating under live commercial pressure. Scope covers theme architecture, app/CMS integrations, metafield/metaobject systems, checkout functions, and performance-aware frontend delivery.

Delivery Context

  • Delivery across live Shopify storefronts with continuous merchandising and campaign release cycles.

Technical Capabilities

Specialist Shopify Plus work beyond the storefront, from platform migrations and B2B to headless builds, integrations and release pipelines.

Shopify Migrations and Replatforming

Moving stores from WordPress, Wix, Salesforce Commerce Cloud (SFCC), Magento and BigCommerce to Shopify Plus.

Migration discovery and technical assessment, data mapping, redirects, SEO preservation and launch planning, covering product, customer, order, content and catalogue migration.

Markets, Multi-Region and B2B

Shopify Markets, multi-region and multi-currency setups, and expansion stores for new territories.

Shopify B2B build-outs covering companies, customer accounts, pricing and permissions.

APIs, Webhooks and Automation

Webhooks, OAuth, app proxies, authentication and rate-limit-aware API integrations.

Workflow automation with Shopify Flow to remove manual steps from store operations.

Headless Shopify

Headless Shopify storefronts built with Remix or Next.js, connected to Shopify through its APIs.

CI/CD and Automated Testing

CI/CD pipelines with GitHub Actions, Theme Check, ESLint and release pipelines for safer deployments.

Testing with Playwright, unit tests and integration tests to catch regressions before launch.

Analytics and Systems Integration

Event tracking and conversion tracking with Shopify Pixels, GA4 and Google Tag Manager (GTM).

ERP, PIM, OMS, WMS and CRM integrations that keep stock, orders and customer data in sync with Shopify.
