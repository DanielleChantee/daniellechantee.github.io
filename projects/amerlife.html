Amerlife Store

Advanced Shopify UX & Performance

The Challenge

Amerlife needed a storefront that could handle complex pricing and variant logic while delivering a fast, conversion-led experience. The theme required advanced interactive components without compromising accessibility or performance.

The Solution

Developed and optimised a custom Shopify theme with dynamic pricing updates, variant-aware product forms, and real-time discount handling. Built interactive UI components including Tolstoy video carousels, responsive buy buttons, and asynchronous variant re-rendering using Liquid and JavaScript. Focused on clean, performance-optimised code and accessibility best practices.

The Outcome

A smoother purchase journey with accurate pricing logic, faster interactions, and a more engaging product experience. The theme now supports advanced merchandising while maintaining a stable, scalable foundation.
