Vantrue

Bespoke Shopify Theme Build

The Challenge

Vantrue required a storefront that reflected its premium product range and brand positioning. The brief called for a highly bespoke build with custom sections, pixel-accurate design implementation, and a strong focus on mobile performance.

The Solution

Developed a custom Shopify theme architecture with sections built from scratch to support advanced layouts and modular content. Implemented a mobile-first UI with performance-led styling, ensuring design fidelity across devices while keeping the codebase clean and scalable.

The Outcome

A polished, brand-aligned storefront with flexible content controls, improved performance, and a robust theme foundation that supports ongoing growth and merchandising.
