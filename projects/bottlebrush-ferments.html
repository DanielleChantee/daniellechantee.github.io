Bottlebrush Ferments

Shopify Theme Development Case Study

Executive Overview

Bottlebrush Ferments required custom Shopify theme delivery aligned to a specialist product range and distinct brand direction. The engagement focused on building a responsive storefront foundation that remained easy to evolve as product and content needs changed.

Core Challenge

The challenge was implementing a tailored storefront experience while keeping the theme practical for ongoing updates, consistency across breakpoints, and maintainable long-term structure.

My Role and Ownership

I owned Shopify theme implementation, including responsive frontend behaviour and custom theme structure aligned to product presentation and brand requirements.

Architectural Contributions

  • Implemented custom Shopify theme structures tailored to brand and product presentation needs.
  • Delivered responsive frontend behaviour across key storefront templates.
  • Maintained clear, manageable theme structure for ongoing content and merchandising updates.

Business and Commercial Outcomes

The outcome was a cleaner, brand-aligned storefront with improved day-to-day maintainability and a stronger base for future iteration.

Why This Matters

Smaller specialist catalogs still need disciplined theme implementation. Maintainable structure and responsive consistency support both customer experience quality and internal delivery efficiency.
