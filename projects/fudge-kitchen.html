Fudge Kitchen

Shopify Engineering Case Study

Executive Overview

Fudge Kitchen is a premium retail brand that required its Shopify storefront to reflect brand quality while remaining practical for ongoing campaign and content updates.

Core Challenge

The existing theme constrained layout flexibility and included defects affecting device-level presentation and performance. The team also needed stronger control over dynamic content without relying on developers for routine edits.

My Role and Ownership

I owned implementation across Liquid sections, templates, and theme settings, including defect resolution, layout refinement, and CMS-app integration for production use.

Architectural Contributions

Theme Flexibility and Stability

  • Implemented direct theme-level improvements across sections and templates to improve layout control.
  • Resolved defects impacting display consistency and performance across device contexts.

Content Workflow Enablement

  • Integrated app-supported dynamic content workflows into custom sections.
  • Improved editorial autonomy so routine content updates could be handled without ongoing developer dependency.

Business and Commercial Outcomes

The outcome was a more reliable, brand-aligned storefront with stronger day-to-day content operations, improved cross-device consistency, and a cleaner foundation for ongoing merchandising work.

Why This Matters

Premium brands need storefront quality and operational control in parallel. This engagement reflects Shopify engineering that improves both user-facing experience and internal delivery efficiency.
