Adios Plastic

Shopify Theme Development

Executive Overview

Adios Plastic required Shopify theme implementation aligned to its sustainability-led brand direction. The engagement focused on custom layouts and product presentation patterns that were accessible, responsive, and practical for ongoing storefront updates.

Core Challenge

The challenge was implementing design-led customisations across Shopify sections and templates while preserving usability and consistency across desktop and mobile storefront journeys.

My Role and Ownership

I owned theme implementation from design-to-code through production-ready delivery. Scope included custom section/template work, responsive layout behaviour, and accessibility-aware product presentation.

Architectural Contributions

  • Translated approved Figma direction into responsive Shopify sections and templates.
  • Implemented custom UX patterns aligned to brand identity and product storytelling.
  • Delivered mobile-first behaviour across key storefront surfaces for cleaner navigation and consistency.

Business and Commercial Outcomes

The outcome was a more coherent brand-aligned storefront with stronger cross-device experience quality and maintainable theme structures for future updates.

Why This Matters

For growing brands, theme implementation quality affects both conversion experience and internal delivery speed. Durable section/template structures reduce friction in day-to-day merchandising.
