The Challenge

QIDI Tech needed a content-led article experience for both EU and global stores, with flexible layouts and clear editor controls. The existing theme lacked a structured way to present long-form content with navigation and visual hierarchy.

The Solution

Built a fully responsive, editor-configurable article template using Liquid, SCSS, and JavaScript. Features include a scroll-linked table of contents, toggleable excerpt view, content-first or content-last layout options, and an optional article frame overlay. Implemented section schema settings and clean template targeting to keep the Shopify Theme Editor intuitive for non-technical teams.

The Outcome

A refined content experience that matches QIDI’s visual identity, improves readability, and scales across markets. The team can now publish structured, on-brand content quickly without development support.
