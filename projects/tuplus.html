Tuplus Store

Custom Shopify Theme Development

The Challenge

Tuplus required a fully bespoke storefront built from detailed Figma designs, with advanced merchandising, flexible content management, and a mobile-first experience. The build needed to support complex product layouts, collection filtering, and interactive content without sacrificing performance.

The Solution

Developed a custom Shopify theme with reusable Liquid sections and full schema configurability. Implemented tabbed collection filters, dynamic content blocks, interactive image banners, and 3D model integration for product variants. Built a responsive typography system tied to theme settings, and integrated custom metafield rendering for flexible editorial control.

The Outcome

A scalable, high-performance theme that translates design intent precisely across devices. The storefront now supports rich content layouts and advanced merchandising while giving the team full control over updates through the Shopify editor.

Related services: Shopify theme developer, Shopify Liquid developer, and Shopify speed optimisation.
