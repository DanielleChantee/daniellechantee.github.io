Tuplus

Shopify Engineering Case Study

Executive Overview

Tuplus required a fully custom Shopify storefront with advanced merchandising behaviour and editor-friendly control. The engagement focused on building a robust theme system that could support growth, evolving catalog needs, and mobile-first user expectations.

Core Challenge

The build needed to combine high-fidelity custom design implementation with complex theme behaviour, including collection interaction patterns, dynamic product presentation, and flexible content management. The key challenge was shipping this without creating brittle template logic.

My Role and Ownership

I owned end-to-end Shopify implementation across architecture decisions, Liquid section and template development, responsive frontend behaviour, and production-focused execution. Scope included translating design into maintainable theme structures and implementing advanced merchandising logic.

Architectural Contributions

Custom Theme System

  • Built a fully custom Shopify theme using reusable sections and schema-driven configurability.
  • Implemented structured template patterns to keep content and merchandising updates maintainable.
  • Delivered design-to-code implementation across desktop and mobile breakpoints.

Merchandising and Product Interaction

  • Implemented advanced collection filtering and tabbed navigation patterns for product discovery.
  • Delivered dynamic content blocks and rich product presentation features, including 3D model integration where required.
  • Supported custom metafield-driven rendering for flexible editorial and product content control.

Mobile-First and Performance-Aware Delivery

  • Built responsive typography and layout behaviour aligned to theme settings and content flexibility.
  • Made implementation decisions that preserved storefront responsiveness while supporting advanced interactions.

Business and Commercial Outcomes

The outcome was a scalable custom storefront foundation that supports richer merchandising, cleaner content operations, and reliable iteration as product and campaign needs evolve.

Why This Matters

For growth ecommerce teams, custom build quality is defined by long-term maintainability as much as launch output. This engagement reflects Shopify engineering that balances flexibility, operational control, and release reliability.
