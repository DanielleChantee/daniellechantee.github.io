Ottocast

Shopify UX Debugging & Navigation

The Challenge

Ottocast’s mega menu was displaying inconsistent imagery across product and collection links. URL parameters and varying page structures caused unreliable data retrieval, affecting visual consistency and user experience.

The Solution

Refactored URL handling logic to ensure reliable product and collection data retrieval regardless of parameters. Modernised image loading techniques to align with Shopify OS 2.0 standards, added robust fallback handling, and improved accessibility across desktop and mobile navigation.

The Outcome

A stable, performance-optimised navigation experience with consistent imagery, improved SEO signals, and a cleaner codebase that scales with ongoing content updates.
