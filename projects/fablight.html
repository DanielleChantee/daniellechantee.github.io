Fablight

Shopify Theme Enhancement Case Study

Executive Overview

Fablight required Shopify theme enhancement to better support lighting and outdoor product merchandising. The engagement focused on expanding theme flexibility and strengthening product presentation capability across key storefront templates.

Core Challenge

The existing theme constrained layout options and limited how product details could be surfaced. The challenge was extending theme behaviour for richer product presentation while preserving compatibility with existing integrations and content structures.

My Role and Ownership

I owned theme enhancement implementation across Liquid sections, schema configuration, and frontend presentation logic. Scope included custom functionality delivery, layout refinement, and compatibility checks with app and metafield usage.

Architectural Contributions

  • Expanded theme flexibility using custom sections and schema-driven configuration patterns.
  • Implemented product presentation features including variant handling, specification blocks, and category-aware layout behaviour.
  • Refined typography and spacing patterns to improve clarity across product and collection contexts.

Business and Commercial Outcomes

The outcome was improved merchandising control and stronger storefront presentation quality, supported by maintainable theme structures the team could continue iterating on.

Why This Matters

Theme flexibility is a commercial requirement for catalog-led stores. Well-structured customisation enables faster merchandising execution without degrading maintainability.
