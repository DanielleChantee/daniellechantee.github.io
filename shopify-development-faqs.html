Frequently Asked Questions

This page covers the most common questions from teams hiring for Shopify contractor, freelance Shopify developer, and ongoing Shopify development support.

What is the difference between contractor and freelance Shopify support?

Contractor work is usually scoped around fixed delivery windows and milestones. Freelance support is usually more flexible and adapts to changing sprint needs.

Do you work on existing Shopify themes?

Yes. Most projects are delivered within existing Shopify 2.0 themes, with maintainable updates to sections, templates, and frontend logic.

Can you support both agencies and in-house ecommerce teams?

Yes. Support is available for agency teams, in-house product teams, and founder-led ecommerce teams.

Can support start as a short project and move into a retainer?

Yes. If delivery priorities continue after an initial scope, support can move into a retainer model for ongoing implementation.

Do you support Shopify Plus implementation?

Yes. Shopify Plus support is available for implementation, theme architecture, and technical delivery planning where larger operational requirements are involved.

Do you only work with UK-based teams?

No. I work with both UK and international teams. Engagements are managed with clear communication, practical scope definition, and direct delivery ownership.
